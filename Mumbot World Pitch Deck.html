A soft

Mumbot World is the home of ghosts, forest spirits, mushroom beings, moss creatures, crystal spirits, skulls and bees — beings that feel both playful and slightly mysterious.

The tone is gentle, curious, cozy, a little haunted. Not horror, even when ghosts and skulls appear. More like a living ecosystem of friendship, growth, nature, memory and imagination.

V · GAMEPLAY
FOUR PILLARS
WHAT YOU DO IN MUMBOT WORLD

Explore · Befriend · Solve · Unlock.

No combat, no fail-states, no leaderboard. Mumbot World is built around four soft loops — quiet activities that reward attention rather than reaction.

01

Explore the forest.

Wander a hand-built ancient woodland of clearings, glades, caves and stranger places. Every corner has been touched by hand.

02

Befriend nature spirits.

Listen, gift, sit awhile. Each spirit has its own rhythm — earn trust and they will open small pockets of the world to you.

03

Solve quiet mysteries.

Puzzles woven into the ecosystem itself — moss that remembers, mushrooms that count, stones that hum on the right night.

04

Unlock the portals.

A portal in a tree leads to the dying distant future. What you carry through, and what you bring back, is up to you.

Mumbot World began as an Unreal Engine demo in 2021. Life got in the way, we paused, we picked it back up. Today the project lives on the web — built on three.js, easy to drop into and shared from day one.

and multiplayer from the ground up.

2025

THE HUB IS BECOMING A VENUE
WHERE THE DESIGN IS MOVING

From game loop
to living hub.

The core pillars stay intact, but the format is evolving from a solo demo into a shared online place: timed gatherings, artist drops, unlockable moments, and listening rooms for music and podcasts.

01

Shared online space.

A low-friction place to meet, wander, sit by the fire, and feel present together.

02

Unlockables.

Soft rewards tied to exploration, events, collaborations, and returning to the world.

03

Timed events.

Seasonal happenings, campfire moments, and live windows that make the forest feel alive.

04

Artist collaborations.

Guest artists can leave objects, rituals, sounds, and temporary places inside the hub.

05

Listening spaces.

Music and podcast rooms built for hanging out, not scrolling past.

06

WIP map.

The sketch is the operating map: a flexible venue layered on top of the world.

XII · TECH & PLATFORM
BUILT FOR EVERYONE

No download.
Just a URL.

We rebuilt Mumbot World on the open web so that anyone, on any device, can step into the forest. Easy to drop into and built to hang out in together — those aren't features, they're the foundation.

RUNTIME · 01

three.js on the open web.

A WebGL runtime that runs in any modern browser — desktop, laptop, tablet, low-end machines. Share the world with a link.

rendererthree.js / WebGL2
deliveryweb · no install
devicesdesktop · tablet · mobile
assetshandcrafted 3D · spirits
EASE OF ACCESS · 02

No install. No account.

You don't download a launcher, you don't make a login, you don't queue for a server. Click a link, you're in. Works on the laptop you already own, the phone in your pocket, the friend's screen you're borrowing.

XVII · ROADMAP
WHERE THE GRANT TAKES US

A patient
release plan.

We don't ship to a marketing calendar — we ship when the world is ready. Here's how Phase 01 unfolds, and where it leads.

PHASE 01NOW · 0–6 MO

First demo, joinable.

  • Playable web demo people can drop into via a link
  • Core loop: explore · befriend · sit by the campfire
  • Frictionless web access & multiplayer foundations in place
  • First character voices & music recorded
PHASE 026–12 MO

Collaborations & events.

  • Guest artists & brand collabs bring unique events to the world
  • Collectible/viewable unlockables tied to each collab
  • Seasonal happenings — campfire gatherings, listening sessions
  • Community joins as participants, not customers
PHASE 0312–24 MO

A growing platform.

  • Steady stream of updates & new regions of the forest
  • External support to keep the lights on between collabs
  • Stay true to the “come chill” feeling at scale
  • Reach a “happy place” for this format
PHASE 0424 MO +

Onward, deeper in.

  • Optional: evolve toward a more gamified experience
  • Or stay as a sanctuary platform — both endings are good ones
  • Decision driven by community, not roadmap pressure
