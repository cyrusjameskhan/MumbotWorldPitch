X · POTENTIAL REVENUE
PROPOSED MODELS TO TEST

Potential
revenue streams

We plan to test collectible releases and paid creative partnerships as ways to support the world.

01

Collectibles

Collectors buy physical editions and, later, optional digital items rooted in Mumbot’s characters.

Figures, plush and prints; matching keepsakes or cosmetics when saved ownership is ready.

02

Creative
partnerships

Brands, galleries and labels commission gatherings, guest spaces and artist releases.

A production fee with a defined scope, hosting period and agreed sales share where relevant.

03

Installations

Venues and event organizers pay to adapt and present the world in physical spaces.

Gallery launches, art-toy fairs and music events, including setup and support.

First tests: one collectible release and one paid partner experience.

Later possibilities: patron memberships, workshops and additional digital purchases, guided by repeat visitation.

XIII · DELIVERY PLAN
PROPOSED SCHEDULE · FROM FUNDING

Phase 01
delivery plan

A six-month plan for a joinable web demo, with reviewable milestones for the core experience and shared play.

MONTHS 0–2

The core forest

Build the first playable route, introduce the characters and establish the campfire interactions.

MilestoneA playable walkthrough of the core experience.
MONTHS 3–4

Shared play

Develop joinable sessions and a first gathering format. Integrate agreed music and voice work.

MilestoneA hosted playtest with multiple visitors.
MONTHS 5–6

Test & open

Test supported devices, improve performance and prepare the first community pilot.

MilestoneA joinable demo with playtest findings and operating-cost estimates.
AFTER PHASE 01

Test a collectible release and a paid partner experience. Use repeat visits, feedback and delivery costs to decide which parts of the hub to expand.

APPENDIX A6 · JOURNEY
FROM MYCELIUM → WEB

Development
milestones

The early Unreal demo established the world’s look and atmosphere. Development now focuses on a browser format and a first joinable community experience.

2021–2022

First seed

Initial Mumbot World Game Demo in Unreal Engine. The mood, the protagonist, the portal-in-a-tree all take shape.

2023–2024

Art & lore

Interactive development pauses while concept art, lore and music continue.

2025

Re-awakening

Development resumes with a focus on making the world easier to enter and share.

2026

Pivot to web

Browser development on three.js, carrying the forest’s visual direction into a new format.
